Investment Guide

Three kinds of clients, one approach: buy to live, buy to invest, or buy for rental income. Use the calculator to test the numbers — then talk to an advisor before deciding.

Buying process

  1. Choose a property and send an inquiry (WhatsApp or the form).
  2. Discuss budget, payment method and possession timeline.
  3. Verify price, ownership and approvals before any payment.
  4. Complete the purchase through the proper legal channel.

Payment plans explained

Ready-to-move properties are usually cash or eligible bank finance. Off-plan projects usually take a booking amount, a down payment, then monthly installments until possession. Rental-income properties are judged on rent versus price — see the calculator.
