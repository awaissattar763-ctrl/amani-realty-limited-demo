Investment Guide

Three kinds of buyers, one approach: buy to live, buy off-plan, or buy for rental income. Use the calculator to test the numbers — then talk to an advisor before deciding.

Buying process

  1. Choose what you are looking for and fill the inquiry form.
  2. Verify your number with the OTP step.
  3. Discuss budget, payment method and possession timeline.
  4. Verify price, ownership and approvals before any payment.
  5. Complete the purchase through the proper legal channel.

Payment plans explained

Ready-to-move properties are usually cash or eligible bank finance. Off-plan projects usually take a booking amount, a down payment, then monthly installments until possession. Rental-income properties are judged on rent versus price — see the calculator.
